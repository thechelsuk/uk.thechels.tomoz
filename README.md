## What is Tomoz?

Tomoz is a focused "tomorrow view" for your iPhone. Instead of digging through a
busy calendar, you get one clean preview of tomorrow's events — work, personal,
and family — so you can plan tonight and start tomorrow ready.

## Features

- **See the day ahead** — tomorrow's events from your chosen calendars in one focused view.
- **Pick and colour-code calendars** — choose which calendars to include and tell them apart at a glance.
- **Reminders add-on** — unlock Reminders with a one-time £1.99 in-app purchase to see tasks alongside your events.
- **Take it with you** — export your plan as Markdown, JSON, or CSV.
- **Siri & Shortcuts** — ask Siri for a spoken summary or automate an export with Apple Shortcuts.
- **Private by design** — a calm, distraction-free layout that keeps the focus on what's next.
